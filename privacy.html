Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what BLUPRNT ("we," "us") collects when you use getbluprnt.com and its applications (the "Service"), and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and authentication details, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Subscription & payment information — processed by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not see or store your full card details; Stripe handles payment data directly. We store a Stripe customer reference and your current plan.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as which features you use, to operate and improve the Service.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service, your account, and your subscription tier.
  • To process payments and manage billing (via Stripe).
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain security and improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to run the Service, including Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), and Vercel (hosting). The Service also displays public sports and market data from providers such as MLB and Polymarket. Each third party handles data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies & local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

Data retention

We keep your account and subscription information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing us.

Your choices

You can update or delete your account, and request access to or deletion of your personal data, by contacting us. You can cancel your subscription at any time through the billing portal.
